Physical and Digital Media

Game Development
Published June 14, 2026
Updated June 14, 2026

Overview

Games exist simultaneously as physical objects and digital experiences — and the relationship between those two forms shapes how they're made, sold, preserved, and remembered. This is a placeholder for a longer piece on that tension.

The Physical Side

Placeholder content. The physical artefacts of games — cartridges, discs, manuals, boxes — carry meaning that a download licence does not. They decay, they wear, they can be lost. That fragility is part of what makes them valuable.

Preservation

Placeholder content. Archiving physical media is a different problem from archiving digital data. Neither is solved, but they fail in different ways.

The Digital Side

Placeholder content. Digital distribution removed the shelf and the manual. It also removed the barrier to entry — for players and for developers. Whether that net change is good depends on who you ask and which part of the industry you're looking at.
