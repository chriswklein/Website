About Christopher

I was raised between Illinois and Indiana. Playing video and board games with my family.

After graduating from DePaul University in 2013, I began working as a QA Tester.

For the past 13 years, I have worked on a variety of games and platforms - now representing two parts of me, as a Senior Quality Assurance Analyst and User Experience Developer.

I enjoy setting up effective QA teams and plans from scratch or working to improve established user workflows. My aim is to continuously deliver high quality reports and improve best practices which benefit the entire development team.

I currently reside in Los Angeles, California and am open to Freelance or Fulltime employment! If you are in need of QA or UX assistance, send me a message via the contact information below!
