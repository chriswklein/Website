Design Audit

Role UX Designer
Timeline August 2023 – October 2023
Tools Figma, Notion, JIRA
User Experience Research Accessibility

Overview

A systematic audit of an enterprise product's interface — cataloguing inconsistencies, accessibility gaps, and friction points across 40+ screens to build a prioritised remediation backlog.

The Problem

The product had grown through several ownership changes and lacked a coherent visual or interaction language. Components with similar function behaved differently depending on which team had built them. Accessibility had never been formally evaluated.

Process

Each screen was reviewed against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria and an internal consistency rubric. Issues were catalogued by severity, component, and frequency. Patterns were identified across issue types to support systematic rather than one-off fixes.

Research

Heuristic evaluation across all primary user flows, supplemented by session recordings and support ticket themes to identify pain points that the interface review alone would not surface.

Iteration

The backlog was reviewed in two rounds with the product and engineering leads to validate severity ratings and agree on scope for a v1 remediation sprint.

Outcome

A prioritised backlog of 80+ issues. Twelve critical accessibility violations resolved in the first sprint. The audit rubric was adopted as part of the standard QA handoff checklist.
