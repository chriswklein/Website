Onboarding Redesign

Role UX Designer
Timeline March 2022 – June 2022
Tools Figma, Maze, Notion
User Experience Prototyping Collaboration

Overview

Redesigning the first-run experience for an internal tool used by 200+ artists — from discovery research through prototype validation across five iterative test rounds.

The Problem

New artists joining the studio took an average of three weeks to reach baseline productivity with the internal asset management tool. Onboarding was informal, tribal, and dependent on whoever happened to sit nearby. Knowledge was not documented; it was inherited.

Process

Structured discovery with new joiners and their supervisors to map where onboarding broke down. Identified five critical moments where users consistently lost confidence or made costly mistakes. Designed a first-run flow to address those moments directly.

Research

Eight contextual enquiry sessions with artists at the three-month mark — recent enough to remember the onboarding experience, experienced enough to articulate where it had failed them. Sessions were observed and recorded with permission.

Iteration

Five prototype rounds, each testing a specific hypothesis about the critical moments. Fidelity increased with each round as confidence in the direction grew. Final round tested with a cohort of actual new joiners during their first week.

Outcome

Time to baseline productivity reduced from three weeks to under one week in the pilot cohort. The onboarding flow was adopted as the standard new joiner experience for all studio tools.
