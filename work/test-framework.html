Test Framework

Role Senior QA Analyst
Timeline October 2024 – January 2025
Tools Confluence, TestRail, JIRA
Quality Assurance Process Tools

Overview

Building a lightweight manual test framework for a game studio's QA pipeline — structured test cases, triage criteria, and a shared vocabulary that reduced bug resolution time across three teams.

The Problem

Three QA teams working on the same product used incompatible bug report formats, inconsistent severity definitions, and no shared triage criteria. Bugs were being filed, escalated, and closed without agreement on what any of those actions meant.

Process

Interviewed leads from each team to map current practices. Identified where definitions diverged and where process friction was highest. Drafted a unified framework and piloted it with one team before rolling out across all three.

Research

Reviewed 200 closed bugs to categorise the types of information that were consistently missing or inconsistently recorded. Used those patterns to define the minimum required fields and triage criteria.

Iteration

Three revision rounds with team leads. The final framework was deliberately minimal — the goal was adoption, not comprehensiveness. Every field included has a clear reason; nothing is there for theoretical completeness.

Outcome

Average time from bug filed to triage decision dropped by 40%. Cross-team escalation disputes dropped to near zero within two months of adoption.
